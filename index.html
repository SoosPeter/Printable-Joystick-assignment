Printable Joystick — "Print it, play it": Build your own controller, and play simple and fun games with friends or alone!

Printable Joystick is a physical party game: players print and assemble simple “controllers” (paper and cardboard) that can be upgraded with 3d printing extra parts, then play fast mini-games driven by joystick moves.
